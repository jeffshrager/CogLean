## Summary

CogLean's package dependencies are clean (no violations, no cycles, Mathlib only in ACTR), and most CogLib modules are what the policy asks for: mechanisms and representations that bind their variables. The weak points are concentrated in four places: (1) a **MultiDigit-shaped vocabulary at the center of CogLib's recognize-retrieve composition** — the general `Cog.Route` names its retrieval branch `whole` (as in "whole-problem"), `Phenomena/ConfusionErrors` takes `wholeWrong`/`isWhole` arguments, face naming and view retrieval inherit those names where they are wrong, and MultiDigit then keeps its own parallel `Route`/`trialWith`/`afterRetrieval` plus a two-tier relabelling of every `RR` theorem; (2) **`Phenomena/Combination` is not theory-neutral** — its phenomena are stated over the Smith–Osherson contrast similarity on prototypes, and it holds Hampton's threshold model, a mechanism; (3) **representation-level and utility code sits in the wrong module** — `KNet`/`slot`/`parent` live in `Process/ViewApplication`, so categorization and spreading activation import view application; `uniform`/`uniformOf` are byte-identical in two Process modules; Rat/List lemmas are scattered across CogLib and re-proved in CogMatrix and Concepts; (4) **mechanisms and library facts live above CogLib** — the MINERVA and strength memory families, and library facts about `Act` and `Net` in CogMatrix; ViewApplication duplicates Concepts' number domain while declaring an unused dependency on it. Most mechanical promotion, same-name and fan-out flags are noise; the real ones are listed below.

## Findings

### 1. "Whole-problem" vocabulary in CogLib's general recognize-retrieve composition
- Category: leakage
- Severity: high
- Location: `CogLib/CogLib/Process/RecognizeRetrieve.lean:53-56` (`Cog.Route.whole | backup`), `:68-75` (`RR.isWhole`, `RR.wholeSuch`, `RR.wholeWrong`, docstrings "A whole-problem answer"), `:120` (`RR.wholeAfter`); `CogLib/CogLib/Phenomena/ConfusionErrors.lean:36-60` (`wholeWrong`/`isWhole` arguments of `ConfusionErrorPattern`, `ConfidentErrors`, `GradedConfusionPattern`, `GradedConfidentErrors`); `CogLib/CogLib/Process/ViewRetrieval.lean:61-62,120-133`.
- Evidence: "whole" only means something against a decomposition — whole problem vs. its columns — which is MultiDigit's (`MultiDigit/MultiDigit/Postulates.lean:90-93`, `Route.whole | columns`). In CogLib the alternative is called `backup`, so `whole` is a leftover for "answered by retrieval". Other users are forced into it: `vtrial` tags an answer read off a view-revised theory as `.whole` (`ViewRetrieval.lean:62`); FaceNaming states "Bob" as a `.whole` answer and "don't know" as `.backup` (`Experiments/Experiments/FaceNaming.lean:117,148-160`); `DeviceLearning/Session.lean:149` passes `RR.wholeWrong`/`RR.isWhole`. The phenomenon module, meant to name no mechanism, bakes the word into its argument names.
- Proposed patch: Rename `Cog.Route.whole` → `Cog.Route.retrieved` (or `direct`); `RR.isWhole` → `RR.isRetrieved`, `RR.wholeSuch` → `RR.retrievedSuch`, `RR.wholeWrong` → `RR.retrievedWrong`, `RR.wholeAfter` → `RR.retrievedAfter`, and the four `Route.*_beq_*` lemmas accordingly; rename the `ConfusionErrors` arguments to `retrievedWrong`/`isRetrieved` and docstrings "whole-problem answer" → "answer stated by retrieval". Theorem names `whole_prob`, `whole_depends_only_on_route` etc. become `retrieved_prob` etc. Update FaceNaming, DeviceLearning/Session, ViewRetrieval, PrimedRecognition and MultiDigit's AnyMemory relabel lemmas (`isWhole_relabel` etc.); MultiDigit keeps "whole" in its own vocabulary (Finding 2). Effect: purely a rename, no statement changes; removes the only task word from CogLib identifiers and makes the `ConfusionErrorPattern` signature read neutrally.
- Prior: new
