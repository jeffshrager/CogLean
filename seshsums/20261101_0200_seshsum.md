# 2026-11-01 02:00: the theory-revision loop made abstract

The user asked how much of the new CogLib code was specific to microwaves or keyed devices, and pointed out that the general level should need only actions and expected responses.

## The answer
No CogLib code mentioned microwaves. But the loop (`Process/TheoryRevision`) hard-wired three things:
- the theory was a knowledge net;
- prediction was function-slot denotations;
- revision was view retrieval.

## The fix
The code is now in three layers, and no result changed:
1. **`Process/TheoryRevision`** is abstract. A `Reviser Θ M Act Resp C B` supplies:
   - `predicts : Θ → List Act → Option Resp`;
   - `revise : C → Θ → M → Dist (Θ × M × B)`;
   - `cues` and `once`.

   Episodes are `List Act × Resp`. The decision-cycle instance is unchanged. Its theorems are `impasse_iff_fails`, `correct_theory_stable`, `mem_fixed` (now conditional on the reviser) and `revise_adopts`. A grep finds no `KNet`, views, slots or denotations in the file.
2. **`Process/ViewRevision`** (new) is the view-application reviser. It has `viewReviser`, `viewReviser_theory` (equal to `retrieveView`), `viewReviser_mem` and `viewReviser_brings`. `revise_same_of_determinate` moved here.
3. **`Process/TheoryExecution`** is documented as one model of acting. It speaks of actions, not keys.

`Phenomena/DeviceLearning` was renamed `Phenomena/TheoryChange`.

`DeviceLearning/Session.lean` now builds its learners with `viewReviser`. All of its theorems and values re-check unchanged.
