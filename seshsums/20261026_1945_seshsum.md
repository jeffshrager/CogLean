## Further problem features (commits 5acdc88, 9df450e)

- **CogLib**:
  - `Determines`: features that fix a property of what is coded;
  - `Net.confusion_preserves`: a confusion keeps every property that its path's features determine (Lemma C).
- **MultiDigit, M1a–M1c** (`Features.lean`), as laws an encoding may satisfy:
  - `Zeros`: whether a digit is 0;
  - `Magnitude`: nine thresholds per digit; two digits differ on exactly |d − e| of them;
  - `ColumnFacts`: an unordered fact per column, which determines the column's sum.
- **Predictions 13–18** (`FeaturePhenomena.lean`):
  - confusions keep zeros;
  - a confusion with a round problem is exact;
  - confused digits lie within the same tested threshold gap;
  - with all column facts tested, a confusion is harmless;
  - with the low columns' facts tested, errors are multiples of 100 (or 10).

  Each has a learned-net version.
- **`Example2.lean`**: a 360-feature encoding that meets all three laws.
  - A tested zero flag keeps 150 + 100 off 100 + 100.
  - Under thresholds, 600 + 100 is taken for its neighbour 700 + 100; under BCD, for 800 + 100.
