## Learning curves (085a38d)

Power and exponential are distinct (`shiftRatio_not_scaleRatio`); an average of two exponential learners is not exponential (`mix_not_shiftRatio`; Estes 1956, Heathcote et al. 2000).
- `CogLib/Process/Practice.lean` with `Act.latency`: ACT-R base level, decay 1. Massed practice tested after a delay is an exact power law (`massed_scaleRatio`). Spaced practice has every qualitative signature of a power law, but no power law fits it over long ranges (`spaced_not_powerLaw`); over trials 1–8 it is within 7.5% of one (`spaced_looks_like_powerLaw`).
