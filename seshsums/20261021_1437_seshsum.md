# Session summary, 2026-10-21 14:37: MultiDigit, EPAM and DOA combined, by deduction

## The idea (Jeff)

Before large-scale models, show the promise of the approach by combining two instances. The combination joins EPAM's recognition to DOA's retrieval, to cover 3-digit addition, where EPAM-style confusions between similar problems arise. Its predictions are to be **deduced from the axiomatized model, not simulated**. No 1000 × 1000 matrix of associations is needed.

Children don't do everything column by column: a familiar problem (100 + 100) is recognized and its answer known. So the model nests DOA one level up:

- **whole-problem retrieval:** the EPAM net recognizes the problem, then DOA retrieval runs on that problem's associations;
- **column backup:** used when the problem isn't recognized or retrieval fails.

## The predictions

1. **Strategy nesting:** column backup with probability 1 if the problem isn't recognized, and (1 − R_q)ⁿ if it is recognized as q.
2. **Whole-problem retrieval depends only on the route:** problems recognized as the same stored problem are answered alike.
3. **A confused problem gets its neighbour's answer,** when the neighbour's associations are sharp (only its sum clears the criterion).
4. **Confident errors.** On the familiar problem, retrieval never errs. On the confused one, every retrieval is wrong, and backup use is just as low. DOA alone cannot produce this: there, errors and backup use come from the same flat distribution. This is the headline prediction.
5. **Same-sum and commuted confusions are harmless.**
6. **Near misses:** within 200 if the path examines the hundreds digits, within 20 with the tens. How far off a confusion is depends on what the familiar set varies on.
7. **Practiced problems stay practiced:** learning other problems leaves a studied problem's trial unchanged.
8. **Similar problems take longer to recognize.**
