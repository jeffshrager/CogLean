This follows the plan "Device learning: view application with retrieved views, on a microwave".

## Decisions
- **No RR bridge** (plan §4). View retrieval has no criterion search over sampled predictions. The phenomenon carries over instead (`view_confusion_pattern`).
- **Memory does not study during revision.** EPAM's escape by learning is therefore deferred to phase 2.
