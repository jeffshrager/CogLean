# 2026-11-01 04:00: decomposed views and type splitting (differentiation)

This follows the user's proposal: split SET-A-TIME and DURATION into SET-A-NUMBER plus NUMBER-IS-A-TIME or NUMBER-IS-A-DURATION, and add type splitting. The sessions use EPAM only, by the user's choice, because the results concern representation.

## CogLib
- **`TheoryExecution`:** `predictT`, whose denotations may read the theory. `predict_eq_predictT` shows the old `predict` is a special case. Also `predictT_congr`.
- **`ViewComposition` (new):** `merge_keeps_unmentioned`. The commutation law is left open.
- **`TypeSplit` (new):**
  - definitions: `splitType`, `retarget`, `Fresh`, `ParentReader`;
  - lemmas about targets, slots and parents after a split;
  - theorems: `split_conservative`, `split_separates`, `same_parent_same_reading` and `predictT_head_congr`.
- **`TheoryRevision`:** the loop records the episodes it has explained (`done`), and `cues` now sees the theory and `done`. Every loop theorem still holds.
- **`ViewRevision`:** `viewSplitReviser`, which aims views at the failing action's type and splits on conflict, recording what was adopted and on which type. Also `viewSplitReviser_mem`.
- **`ViewApplication.matchings`:** a view frame with the same name as a theory frame is reserved for it. BigTrak, Numbers and Selection re-check unchanged.
- **`Phenomena/TheoryChange`:** `oscillates`.

## DeviceLearning
- **`Microwave.lean`:**

- **`Differentiation.lean` (new):** `one_view_not_enough`, `two_views_compose` (either order), `revision_keeps_number`, `net_facts`, `no_split_session` and `split_session`. Over 12 cycles:
