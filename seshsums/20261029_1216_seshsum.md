## Vocabulary: how to assert things in CogLean
- **No `axiom`s.** A Lean `axiom` is assumed without proof, everywhere, by every importer, and is never checked; a false one makes everything suspect. CogLean declares none (only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`), and `gen_matrix.py` rejects cells that use others.
- **Lemmas** are proved stepping stones (`pRet_pair`, `cross`). They add no assumptions.
- **Lean checks, it doesn't decide.** A statement is proved, disproved (its negation proved), or unknown. `decide` settles concrete cases by computing; statements over all parameter values need written proofs.
- **Three ways to assert, loosest to tightest:**
  1. *The model is like this*: a **definition** (strengths only add; the net learns this way). Can't be false or inconsistent.
  2. *Assume the situation is like this*: a **hypothesis**: in each theorem (`hT : total … ≠ 0`), once per section (`variable`), or **bundled** as a field of a structure (`Encoding` carries a proof of M1). Every concrete instance must prove it.
  3. *Pin it down*: **define** the concrete case and **prove** it meets the condition (`bcd`, `net`, `str`; `sharp_q1`, `net_facts`). The result about that case has no conditions left. If the choice fails the condition, the proof fails.
- What the prior discussion called a "construction" is a CogMatrix **family**: a parametrized definition, with properties proved for one setting or for every setting.
