## The integrated multi-digit model (`MultiDigit/MultiDigit/Integrated.lean`)

The request: `Example.lean`'s backup, `Dist.pure p.sum`, hands over the answer. Compose the existing mechanisms so that nothing does.

**The model (composition only, no new arithmetic).**
- `solverBackup F p := Solver.answers F.fact Solver.full Solver.lenient p`: the column solver on CogLib's decision cycle, each column a DOA trial (`doaFact`).
- `itrial := MD.trial E N str as cc n (solverBackup F)`: EPAM recognition, then whole-problem DOA retrieval, then the solver when retrieval fails.
- Correct knowledge enters only in the single-digit associations, which are peaked on each pair's sum, and in the carry rule.

**Results, by kind.** The file header holds the table.
- *Composition, general:*
  - `solverBackup_eq_columns` (via `bridge`);
  - `mass_solverBackup`;
  - `whole_independent_of_solver`: a better solver cannot repair a whole-problem error;
  - `columnsSuch_prob`: the column route factors as exhaustion × the solver;
  - `solver_error_is_doa_error`.
- *Conditional:*
  - `integrated_confident_errors`;
  - `errors_two_sources`: whole-route answers are always wrong, while column-route errors are the solver's DOA-driven errors, scaled by exhaustion;
  - `solver_exact_of_exact`: exact only given exact facts, which DOA does not supply.

  Their conditions are confusion (derived from the learned net), sharp associations (an empirical premise) and proper strengths (well-formedness).
- *Setting:* Example's net and strengths. The DOA rule is the same for every digit pair: sum 4/5, next count 3/25, the rest 1/100, criterion 1/10, search 1, and the Sims defaults for e, qs and qd. 150 + 100:
  - whole route says 200 with probability 45/46, and the solver runs with probability 1/46;
  - the solver gives 250 ≈ .656, 260 ≈ .099, 350 ≈ .097, 251 ≈ .095;
  - overall, 200 ≈ .978 and 250 ≈ .0143.

  100 + 100 is correct overall ≈ .993.
